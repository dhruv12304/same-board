Chess Council Unveils New Title Proving Girls Can Achieve Anything, Minus 200 Rating Points

Tuesday · Satire

LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND—In a landmark effort to show girls they belong at the chessboard, the Global Chess Equity Council announced Tuesday a women-only title requiring 200 fewer rating points than the one men earn.

"We want every girl to know she can reach the very top, or at least a nearby spot we've reserved for her," said council director Margaret Hollis, adding that the certificate would be printed in a slightly smaller font.

Hollis also praised the council's rule requiring every team to field at least one woman, and clarified that an all-women team would now need to recruit a man "to keep things balanced."

Asked about research finding that roughly 96 percent of the gap among top players is explained by how many women play in the first place, Hollis said the council was "deeply committed to every solution except more girls."

At press time, the council had created a new trophy for the girl who most graciously accepted being told she was "really good, for a girl."

Funny? Parts of it are real.

The 200-point gap between titles is real. So is the team rule that told Britain's only all-women team to add a man. See the rules, the research, and what they really say about women in chess.
